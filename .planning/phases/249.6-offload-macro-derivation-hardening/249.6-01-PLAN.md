| T-249.6-02 | Tampering | `owned_type` accepting an unsupported `&&T` receiver form | accept (now compile-blocked) | WR-01 rejects the form at the `#[offload]` span at compile time; no malformed payload type can reach runtime. No runtime attack surface — input is developer source, resolved before any deploy. |

No high/medium threats are introduced.
