## Decisions Made
- Intent-agnostic design: `_intents` and `_ctx` ignored — template context is the same regardless of inferred intent. This is by design (D-01 through D-08 from research).
- Used `serde_json::Map` explicitly rather than a `HashMap` to guarantee the output is a JSON object type, not an array.
- `state_machine` serializes as `null` rather than being absent — template engines benefit from a predictable shape.
