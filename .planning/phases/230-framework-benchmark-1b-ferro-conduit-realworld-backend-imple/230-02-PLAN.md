<objective>
Define the seven Conduit tables and their SeaORM entities + migrations: users, articles, comments, tags, plus the three junction tables (article_tags, follows, favorites). This is the data spine every endpoint wave queries.

Purpose: Conduit's relational shape (M:N follows/favorites/tags, 1:N comments) maps onto SeaORM 1.0 (RESEARCH §2). Direct junction-table queries are the chosen approach over the `Linked` trait (RESEARCH line 202 — lower risk, more readable).

Output: Migrations that create all tables against Postgres, and entities the controllers query.
</objective>
