<objective>
Refactor the Renderer trait in ferro-projections from visual-only to modality-agnostic using associated types, split the monolithic RenderContext into BaseContext (modality-agnostic) and VisualContext (visual-only), and gate ferro-theme behind an optional `visual` feature flag.

Purpose: Unblocks v12.0 (visual renderer rewrite) and v14.0 (non-visual renderers like WhatsApp, voice). Without this, channel adapters would be bolted on to a visual-only trait.

Output: A ferro-projections crate whose core Renderer trait is output-agnostic, compilable without ferro-theme.
</objective>
