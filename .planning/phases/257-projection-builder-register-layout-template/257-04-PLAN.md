<objective>
Close the single major UAT gap on the projection-derived `/cassa` register: under
`fill_viewport`, the SelectionPanel does not pin — its Total row and "Conferma
ordine" button render off-viewport (y≈1032–1125 in a 746px viewport) and both panes
scroll together as one block, breaking the 256 D-15 pinned-footer contract.

Root cause (diagnosed live in Chrome DevTools, evidence in 257-HUMAN-UAT.md Gaps
YAML): `emit_register_root` composes root `Grid(fill:true)` → `Form#sale_form` →
inner `Grid(h-full min-h-0)` → panes. The outer fill-Grid cell is correctly
height-constrained (673px, `overflow-y-auto`), but `render_form` emits
`flex flex-wrap` with NO height-chain classes, so the Form is content-sized (1076px)
and the inner Grid's `h-full` resolves against 1076px instead of 673px. The Form was
never in the fill chain before 257 — the hand-authored `cassa.json` kept its confirm
form as a small leaf inside the cart pane. The 256 D-11 Form-as-common-ancestor
composition (LOCKED — hidden inputs must stay inside the Form scope) puts the Form in
the height chain, and `render_form` has no way to emit fill/height classes.

Fix (validated concrete): additive `FormProps.fill: Option<bool>`; `render_form`
emits a fill height-chain class string when true; `emit_register_root` sets
`fill: true` on the sale_form. The Form-as-common-ancestor composition is unchanged.

Purpose: restore the pinned header/total/confirm contract on the projection-derived
register so the operator can always see and reach the running total and confirm
button (POS-10 killer-feature quality bar — one ServiceDef → a working tablet sale
screen).
Output: an additive, backward-compatible Form fill prop; a fill-aware form renderer;
the register projector wired to it; regression coverage at the render, projection,
and app-e2e layers; regenerated `ferro-base.css` if the new class combos surface.
</objective>
