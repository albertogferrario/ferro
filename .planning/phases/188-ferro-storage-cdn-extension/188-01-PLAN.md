Add the CDN-URL presentation layer to ferro-storage and lay the Cargo.toml foundation that Plans 02/03 build on. This is the cheap enabler half of the phase (STOR-F-01, success criterion 1) plus the dependency/feature scaffolding (Wave 0 for criterion 4).
