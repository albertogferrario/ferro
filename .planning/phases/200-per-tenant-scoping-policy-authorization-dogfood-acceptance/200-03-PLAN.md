<objective>
Stand up the minimal two-tenant data substrate in the sample `app` (D-07): a `tenants` table, an
`orders` table whose columns exactly match the `order` projection's declared field names plus a
`tenant_id` FK, a nullable `tenant_id` on `users`, and the SeaORM models + custom wrappers
(`Tenant::find_by_id`/`find_by_slug`, `Order`). Without this, SC-1 isolation is unprovable —
the app currently has no tenants and no orders table.

Purpose: The data prerequisite for tenant scoping and the dogfood. Pure data layer — no
middleware, no auth, no routes (those land in Plans 04/05).
Output: Three migrations registered, two new entity models + wrappers, users entity gains the
tenant_id field, re-exports wired.
</objective>
