## Overview

Touch-first sale-screen components in the ferro-json-ui builtin catalog — TileGrid (with
integrated search + filter strip), SelectionPanel, FilterTabs, QuantityStepper, Numpad — at
a tablet interaction quality bar, derivable from a `ServiceDef` through the Collect/Register
projection layer and agent-authorable through the v16.5 MCP + design-lint boundary. Seed
finding: the ~1500-line RawHtml product picker audited in `253-FRICTION.md`.

**Scope constraints:**
- All POS components are **builtins** — any `register_component` use is a review blocker.
- The seven-intent vocabulary is unchanged — `Register` is a layout-template arm under
  **Collect**, not a new intent.
- Form-state accumulation stays (Tile hidden inputs → single confirm POST); the SelectionPanel
  is a live client-side view of that form state (the CartRuntime slice was un-deferred into
  Phase 256, operator decision 2026-07-05; tiles are tap-to-add-only, all quantity editing in
  the SelectionPanel — Shopify POS model).
- **Domain-neutral vocabulary** (operator decision 2026-07-05): the builtin catalog carries
  structural nouns only (`Tile`, `TileGrid`, `SelectionPanel`, `FilterTabs`); commerce naming
  is confined to sample apps + docs; no consumer-specific props in any `ferro-*` crate. Renames
  applied pre-publish in Phase 255 (`ProductTile`→`Tile` is a break of the published component,
  documented in the migration table).
- No new crates; single crates.io publish at Phase 258.

**Out of scope:** payment flow, receipt rendering, shift/session close, hardware integration
beyond keyboard-wedge (deferred).

## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**

- **Domain-neutral catalog vocabulary** — structural nouns only in `ferro-*` crates; commerce
  naming confined to sample apps + docs. `ProductTile`→`Tile` breaking rename applied pre-publish.
- **Register is a layout template under Collect**, not a new intent — the seven-intent
  vocabulary stays frozen.
- **Form-state cart + live client-side SelectionPanel** — tiles tap-to-add-only; quantity
  editing in the panel; single confirm POST; no per-tap server round-trips.
- All POS components are builtins (never plugins); zero new dependencies.

**Technical Debt / Deferred:** barcode keyboard-wedge input; a layout-name-independent
`ferro-fill` chain (the `fill-viewport-layout-unknown` lint rule mitigates meanwhile).
